* uLisp programming

The [[https://www.ulisp.com][uLisp]] language is a very small Lisp that runs on microcontrollers,
including several ARM, ESP32, and Risc-V types.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  (use-package ulisp-mode
    :load-path "~/.emacs.d/lisp"
    :mode ("\\.ul\\'" . lisp-mode)
    :hook (lisp-mode . ulisp-minor-mode)
    :commands (ulisp-minor-mode ulisp-connect ulisp-setup)
    :custom
    (ulisp-serial-port "/dev/cu.usbmodem214301")
    (ulisp-baud-rate 115200)
    (ulisp-auto-connect nil))
#+end_src
